### TerraGuide AI — Local AI Assistant for Land Administration & GIS

Developed a browser-based AI guide combining a from-scratch character-level transformer, local RAG over GIS/GPS/PDF knowledge, user training rules stored in the browser, spatial SQL generation, and WGS84-to-UTM conversion. Designed for land administration, surveying, geomatics, and spatial analysis.

Developed a Python pipeline that ingests authoritative CO₂ and climate data, validates provenance, decomposes time series, detects change points, forecasts with intervals, scores carbon dioxide removal methods, and exports GIS-ready and report assets.

### Ifc2postGIS

Created a Python CLI and QGIS plugin that converts IFC spatial hierarchy and 3D geometry into PostGIS tables, enabling BIM/GIS interoperability for site, building, storey, and space layers.

### 2D & 3D Coordinate Transformation

### Bulwark — Threat Intelligence & Security Operations Site

### Probe — Exposure & Compliance Scanner
